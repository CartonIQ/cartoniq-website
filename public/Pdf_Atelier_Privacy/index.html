1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the PDF Atelier Android application (the "App"), developed by Mohd Arsh under the brand CartoniQ. We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with global data protection laws, including the GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), and the DPDP Act (India).

2. Data Collection and Local Storage

PDF Atelier is designed with an offline-first architecture.

  • Local Storage: All PDF files, images, annotations, and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not own, operate, or maintain any servers that store your personal documents.
  • No Account Required: The App does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or log in.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all locally stored app data at any time by going to the App's Settings and selecting "Delete My Data" or by uninstalling the App.

3. Third-Party AI Services (Google Gemini)

The App integrates with the Google Gemini API to provide AI features such as chat, summarization, and image analysis.

  • Data Transfer: When you use the AI features, the text or images you provide are sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. This data is subject to Google's Privacy Policy.
  • No Training: We do not use your prompts or documents to train any AI models. Data is processed in real-time for the specific request you make.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

If the free version of the App displays ads, Google AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID and approximate location to serve relevant ads. You can manage ad consent within the App's settings via the User Messaging Platform (UMP).

5. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions to function correctly:

  • Storage/Photos/Media: Required to open, read, edit, and save PDF documents and images on your device.
  • Camera: Required for the "Scan" and "OCR" features to capture documents.
  • Microphone: Required if you use voice-to-text or voice notes.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary. All document data is stored locally on your device and is retained until you delete it or uninstall the App. AI prompts are retained by Google only for the duration of the processing request.

7. Security

We take security seriously. Any sensitive documents saved in the App's secure vault are protected using AES-256 encryption. However, please remember that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure. You are responsible for keeping your device and backups secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

9. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR, CCPA, DPDP)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the data we hold about you (note: we do not store any data on our servers).
  • Right to Erasure: You can delete all local data via the App's settings.
  • Right to Object: You can choose not to use the AI features or disable personalized ads in settings.
