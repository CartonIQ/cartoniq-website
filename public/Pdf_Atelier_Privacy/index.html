Privacy Policy for PDF Atelier

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the PDF Atelier Android application (the "App"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and complying with global data protection laws, including the GDPR (EU), CCPA (California), and the DPDP Act (India).

2. Data Collection and Local Storage

PDF Atelier is designed with an offline-first architecture.

  • Local Storage: All PDF files, images, annotations, and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not own or operate servers that store your personal documents.
  • No Account Required: The App does not require you to create an account, provide an email address, or log in.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all locally stored app data at any time by going to the App's Settings and selecting "Delete My Data" or by uninstalling the App.

3. Third-Party AI Services & Bring Your Own Key (BYOK)

The App integrates AI features using a "Bring Your Own Key" model. You must provide your own API key to use the AI features.

  • Supported Providers: You can choose to connect to Google Gemini, OpenAI, Groq, OpenRouter, or a Custom OpenAI-compatible endpoint.
  • Data Transfer: When you use the AI features, the text or images you provide are sent directly from your device to your chosen AI provider. This data is subject to the respective provider's Privacy Policy (e.g., Google, OpenAI, Groq).
  • Local Key Encryption: Your API keys are encrypted and stored locally on your device only. We do not collect, transmit, or store your API keys on our servers.
  • No Training: We do not use your prompts or documents to train any AI models.

4. Third-Party Advertising (Google AdMob)

If the free version of the App displays ads, Google AdMob may collect your device's Advertising ID and approximate location to serve relevant ads. You can manage ad consent within the App's settings via the User Messaging Platform (UMP).

5. Permissions

The App may request the following permissions to function correctly:

  • Storage/Photos/Media: Required to open, read, edit, and save PDF documents and images on your device.
  • Camera: Required for the "Scan" and "OCR" features to capture documents.
  • Microphone: Required if you use voice-to-text or voice notes.

6. Data Retention

We retain your data only for as long as necessary. All document data is stored locally on your device and is retained until you delete it or uninstall the App. AI prompts are retained by the chosen AI provider only for the duration of the processing request.

7. Security

We take security seriously. Any sensitive documents saved in the App's secure vault are protected using AES-256 encryption. API keys are stored using encrypted local storage. However, please remember that no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

9. Your Data Protection Rights (GDPR, CCPA, DPDP)

Depending on your location, you may have the following rights:

  • Right to Access: You can request a copy of the data we hold about you (note: we do not store any data on our servers).
  • Right to Erasure: You can delete all local data via the App's settings.
  • Right to Object: You can choose not to use the AI features or disable personalized ads in settings.
