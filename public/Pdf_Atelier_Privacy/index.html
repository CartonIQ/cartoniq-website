Privacy Policy for PDF Atelier

Effective Date: November 15, 2026

Developer: Mohd Arsh (CartoniQ)
Contact: cartoniq.business@gmail.com

1. Introduction

This Privacy Policy applies to the PDF Atelier Android application developed by Mohd Arsh. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how your data is handled when you use the App.

2. Data Collection and Storage

PDF Atelier is designed with an offline-first architecture.

  • Local Storage: All PDF files, images, annotations, and app settings are stored locally on your device. We do not operate servers that store your personal documents.
  • No Account Required: The App does not require you to create an account or log in.
  • Data Deletion: You can delete all locally stored app data at any time via the Settings or by uninstalling the App.

3. Third-Party Services & AI Features

  • Google Gemini API: When you use the AI features, the text or images you provide are sent to Google's Gemini API for processing. This data is subject to Google's Privacy Policy. We do not store these prompts.
  • Google AdMob: If ads are displayed, Google AdMob may collect your Advertising ID to serve relevant ads.

4. Permissions

The App may request Storage (to read/save PDFs), Camera (for OCR/Scanning), and Microphone (for voice notes).

5. Security

Sensitive documents in the secure vault are protected using AES-256 encryption. However, no method of electronic storage is 100% secure.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Contact Us

If you have any questions, contact the developer at cartoniq.business@gmail.com.
